<objective>
Fix subscription leak cleanup in GamePage.tsx and LobbyPage.tsx.

Purpose: These two pages already use `useRef` for their channel variable but call `channel.unsubscribe()` instead of `supabase.removeChannel()`. This leaves orphaned channels in the Supabase RealtimeClient registry, causing accumulation on repeated navigation. The fix is mechanical: replace unsubscribe with removeChannel, add null guard, null the ref, and tighten the type from `any` to `RealtimeChannel | null`.

Output: Two files with correct channel cleanup pattern.
</objective>
