<objective>
Fix subscription leak cleanup in ChallengePage.tsx and TournamentPage.tsx.

Purpose: These two pages use a local `const channel` inside `useEffect` rather than `useRef`, and call `channel.unsubscribe()` in cleanup. This leaves orphaned channels in the Supabase RealtimeClient registry. The fix adds `useRef` to the React import, adds a `RealtimeChannel` type import, introduces a `channelRef`, and replaces unsubscribe with `supabase.removeChannel()`.

Output: Two files with correct channel cleanup pattern, consistent with GamePage/LobbyPage.
</objective>
